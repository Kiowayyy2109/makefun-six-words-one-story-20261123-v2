## The story

> **Widow remarried. Her late husband applauded.**

A whole life in six words: a widow finds love again after loss,
and the twist — her late husband applauds — leaves the reader
wondering whether it is a ghost's blessing, her imagination, or a
dark little joke. Everything unsaid is the story.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it
first: exactly 6 words, 1–60 characters, not only whitespace.
